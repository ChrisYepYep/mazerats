    Console

    Who the homepage console credits on its Source page.

    Contributors

    Shown on the homepage console's Source page, most contributions first.

    Messages

    Submitted from the homepage console's Contact Us page. "Ban IP" blocks that address (or its whole home network) — from everything but reading, or from the whole site, for a set time or for good — see the Bans tab.

    Errors

    What broke on visitors' screens and in the site's functions, one row per distinct problem. Press a row for when it happened, who it happened to, the stack, and what the visitor did just before. Times are UTC; hover one for your local time.

    Guides

    How-to guides, read in the Guides window on the homepage (/guides). A guide is a title, a summary and a list of sections, each with its own text and picture. Drafts are only visible here; publishing puts a guide on the site and in What's New.

    What's New

    The log on the homepage, as visitors see it. Mazes, events and guides appear on their own when they're added or edited; Amend changes how one appears (its date, Added or Updated, the line under it). Hide takes any single entry off the homepage, news and notes included, without deleting it. Add news, or a note about a maze or event, with the buttons on the right.

    Each new entry goes to the Maze Rats Discord on its own about half an hour after its last save, so a run of edits goes as one post and there's time to Hide or Amend it first. The Discord button on a row posts one by hand, or again.

    Missing Pieces

    Mazes and events the archive can't finish without help. Tick what's missing and it's listed in the console's Missing Pieces page and on the record itself, with an "I can help" button. Submissions from the console's Add Maze Info form land here, including ones about mazes that aren't in the archive yet. Images people send stay private until you accept them.

    Leads

    What visitors sent through Add Maze Info. Accepting can copy their screenshots into the archive, tick off what they answered, and credit them in Contributors.

    Records

    Only what you tick and save here shows on the site. "Suggestions" lists unmarked records the page thinks have gaps; opening one pre-ticks those, so untick anything that isn't really missing.

    Daily games

    Recolour

    Every colour the site paints with, found by reading the stylesheet that is actually running — so this list is whatever the site looks like today, not a list kept up to date by hand. A palette stores only what you changed, which is why one saved last year still works after the CSS has moved on. The preview is this panel: it is dressed as the site, so what you pick shows on it as you go. For what isn't here — the header and footer, a maze window, the games — press Preview on the site. Load opens any existing skin to edit — a saved palette, or a copy of one of the built-in themes, which saves as a new palette and leaves the theme itself alone.

    Bans

    Every ban and cool-down: on a Discord account, on an address or IPv6 /64, or on the network a signed-in player last came from. Everything but reading lets them browse but not sign in, play, appear on the boards, set a nickname or send anything; Whole site shows them a banned screen on every page but the privacy policy. A cool-down is a ban with an end. Ban a player from their row on the Players tab, or add any ban here. Times are UTC.

    Activity

    What visitors do on the site, and what the admin accounts have done. Owner-only.

    Sign-ins and changes made by admin accounts, kept for 90 days.

    Sessions

    One row per sign-in. "Active for" is the gap between the first and last thing that session did, so it measures work rather than an open tab.

    Everything, newest first

    Visitor figures are counted, never listed: the records carry no address and no account, a "visit" is one browser tab (somebody back tomorrow is a new visit), and search terms are never stored. Anyone sending Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is not counted at all. Kept for 60 days. Times are UTC.

    Fallin' Furni

    Every run that was played, including the ones that went badly — those are the ones that say which level is too long. Signed-in players are named; everyone else is counted as anonymous. Kept for 180 days.

    Level by level

    Sorted by how often each one was reached. A low clear rate with a lot of time-outs means the level is too long; a lot of wrong seats means it is too confusing.

    Players

    One row per signed-in player, one per habbo name given in the game without signing in, and a single line for everyone who gave no name at all. A name typed into the game is what somebody said about themselves, not something this site checked — two rows can be one person, and a name can be anybody's.

    Player Runs Best Best points Time played First seen Last seen

    Addresses

    Runs grouped by the connection they were played from, so several accounts sharing one are visible. A shared address is not a shared person — families, halls, schools, offices and whole mobile networks put unrelated people behind one, and a phone can move between several in a day. Treat a match as a question, never an answer.

    Players

    Event Entries

    Entries sent through Event Submission on the console's Contact page, newest first: the Habbo Origins username they entered under, their picture, and their Discord account if they were signed in. "Entries go to" decides which event a new entry is filed under; on Automatic it is the one event running right now, and none while there are several or none at all.
