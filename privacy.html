Privacy Policy

What this site collects when you use it, what it is used for, how long it is kept, and how to have it removed. It is short because the site does little: there is no advertising, nothing is sold or shared, and signing in is optional — the archive, Fallin' Furni and Pura Panic work fully without an account, while the daily games need one, with a nickname.

Getting in touch

Requests to review or delete anything held about you, and any question about this policy, go through the Contact page in the site's console — the small Habbo console button in the header on the archive. While the archive is closed before it opens, or for maintenance, the console cannot be reached, so you can also ask on the Maze Rats Discord.
