Bans

Addresses blocked from the contact form. A banned sender still sees a normal "sent" confirmation — nothing is saved and no email goes out — so they are not nudged into simply switching address.

Activity

Sign-ins and changes made by admin accounts. Owner-only, and kept for 90 days before it prunes itself.

What visitors use

Counted, never listed: these records carry no address and no account, the session id is discarded when a visitor closes their tab, and search terms are never stored. Anyone sending "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control is not counted at all. Kept for 60 days.

Sessions

One row per sign-in. "Active for" is the gap between the first and last thing that session did, so it measures work rather than an open tab.

Fallin' Furni

Every run that was played, including the ones that went badly — those are the ones that say which level is too long. Signed-in players are named; everyone else is counted as anonymous. Kept for 180 days.

Sorted by how often each one was reached. A low clear rate with a lot of time-outs means the level is too long; a lot of wrong seats means it is too confusing.
